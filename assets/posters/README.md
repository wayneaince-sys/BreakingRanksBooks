# Posters

Vintage scrapbook collage posters featuring Wayne, generated with Perplexity Computer using the uploaded selfie as the facial identity reference. All versions are 9:16 vertical, warm sepia + navy grade, mixed-media aesthetic. No fabricated family members or fictional achievements — every face in every fragment is Wayne himself, and the only additional living subject is his red/apricot Cavapoo.

## Current version

**v6 — Side-profile centerpiece.** `wayne-scrapbook-collage-poster-v6.png`

- Central portrait is a true 90-degree cinematic side profile facing screen-left, warm rim light against a darker background.
- "God is My Anchor" banner across the top with two gold anchor icons on aged cream ribbon.
- Anchor words in warm sepia cursive: Faith, Grace, Purpose, Strength, Resilience, Grateful.
- Verse cards: Philippians 4:13, Proverbs 3:5-6, Joshua 1:9, Jeremiah 29:11 (tucked under the puppy Polaroid), 1 Thessalonians 5:18, plus 2 Corinthians 5:7 on the leather Bible.
- Life fragments: praying with Bible, gym / weights, journaling, driving BMW, military uniform with flag holding coffee, thoughtful portrait, tying running shoes, kneeling silhouette at stained-glass window, walking the red Cavapoo down a tree-lined sidewalk, seated on the wood floor playing with the red Cavapoo puppy (teddy bear + kids' picture books beside — hinting at fatherhood without depicting a child).
- Ephemera: leather Bible "Walk by Faith Not by Sight", brass compass, praying-hands cutout, navy wax seal with gold-embossed Latin cross, gold olive branch, restrained gold-foil accents on aged parchment.

## Generation notes

- Facial identity anchored to the uploaded selfie reference on every pass.
- Central portrait, banner, wax seal, and anchor words are the fixed design system across versions.
- No fabricated children, spouse, or other family members in any fragment. Fatherhood is implied through objects (teddy bear, picture books, the family dog), not through invented people.
- Clothing kept modest and dark (navy, charcoal, deep burgundy, cream) to reflect Christian faith throughout.
